# Sky Jumper - Mobile Game

A fun jumping game you can play on your phone or computer!

## How to Play

1. **Tap the screen** to make your rocket jump
2. **Tap again while in the air** for a double jump
3. **Touch and drag** to move left or right
4. **Collect the gold stars** for bonus points
5. **Don't fall off the bottom!**

## Controls

- **On Phone:** Tap anywhere to jump, touch and hold to move
- **On Computer:** Click to jump, move mouse while clicking to move, or press SPACEBAR to jump

## Tips

- You have 3 lives (the hearts at the top)
- The higher you go, the more points you get
- Stars give you 100 extra points
- The game gets faster as your score goes up

## How to Run

1. Open the `index.html` file in a web browser
2. Press "TAP TO START" to begin
3. Have fun!

## Files

- `index.html` - The main game page
- `style.css` - Makes the game look pretty
- `game.js` - The game code that makes everything work
